Acquisitions are mostly an integration problem. Mobile SDK acquisitions are integration problems where the customer's app binary is the boundary you can't move.

## The three forced choices

Every customer-facing SDK migration eventually presents three choices, in this order:

1. **Coexist or replace?** — Two SDKs in one binary is a debugging nightmare; replacement risks customer churn.
2. **Identity reconciliation** — Whose user ID wins? When? At what merge cost?
3. **API parity vs. ergonomics** — Do you preserve the old surface forever, or break callers and own the migration?

We chose coexist-then-replace, with a 12-month migration window. Worth it. More to come.
